Pins

10 kΩ potentiometer: one end to 3V3, the other to GND, wiper to the pin. Knobs need an ADC1 pin (GPIO 1–10). Volume with no knob attached reads full scale (100 %); the delay knob replaces the delay trim above, centre = 0 ms.
Every pin carries one function, so a pin in use is left out of the other lists. Never offered: . Changing a pin reboots the device. If it then fails to start three times in a row, it goes back to the standard pins.
